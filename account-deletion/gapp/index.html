Gapp Account Deletion

Last updated: August 2026
Gapp is developed by Napp Labs.
If you would like to permanently delete your Gapp account and associated data, please email:
support@napptechlabs.com

Subject: Delete Gapp Account
Please include your Gapp username in your request so that we can identify your account.
The following data will be deleted:
→ Gapp account
→ Username
→ Messages stored by Gapp
Data retention:
Once your identity has been verified, your account and associated data will be permanently deleted. Data may be retained only where required by applicable law or for legitimate security purposes, after which it will also be deleted.
If you have any questions, please contact:
support@napptechlabs.com
