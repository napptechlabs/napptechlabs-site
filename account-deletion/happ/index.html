HAPP Account Deletion

Last updated: November 2026

HAPP is developed by Napp Labs.

If you would like to permanently delete your HAPP account and associated data, please email:

support@napptechlabs.com

Subject: Delete HAPP Account

Please include your HAPP account email address so that we can identify your account.

The following data will be deleted:

→ HAPP account
→ Google Sign-In account association
→ Display name and user profile information
→ Recent interaction records
→ Room presence and associated signalling data

Data retention:

Once your identity has been verified, your account and associated data will be permanently deleted. Data may be retained only where required by applicable law or for legitimate security purposes, after which it will also be deleted.

If you have any questions, please contact:

support@napptechlabs.com
