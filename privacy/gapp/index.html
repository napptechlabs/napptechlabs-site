GAPP Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026
GAPP is developed by Napp Labs. This Privacy Policy explains what information is used when you use GAPP.
Account information
GAPP requires you to create an account using a username and password. Your chosen username is stored to enable messaging between users.
Messages
Messages you send are stored using Google's Firebase Realtime Database so they can be delivered to recipients and synchronized across devices.
Data collection
GAPP stores only the information necessary to provide its messaging service, including:
→ Username
→ Authentication information
→ Messages you send and receive
Third-party services
GAPP uses Firebase services provided by Google for authentication and message storage. Google's handling of data is governed by its own privacy policies.
Data sharing
Napp Labs does not sell your personal information or share it with third parties for advertising purposes.
Permissions used
→ Internet — required for messaging and account authentication.
