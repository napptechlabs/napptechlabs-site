Privacy Policy

HAPP · Napp Labs
Last updated: 16 November 2026

HAPP is a communication application developed by Napp Labs. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information HAPP collects, how it is used, and how it is handled when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use HAPP, we may collect and process the following information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive information associated with your Google account, including your user identifier, display name and email address.
  • Profile Information: Your user identifier and display name are used to create and maintain your HAPP profile.
  • Interaction Information: Information about recent interactions with other users may be stored to support the application's functionality.
  • Room Information: HAPP processes temporary room participation information, including user presence, to indicate whether participants are currently available in a room.
  • Microphone Access: HAPP requires microphone permission to enable voice communication between participants.

2. Voice Communication

HAPP uses WebRTC technology to facilitate real-time voice communication between participants.

Audio is transmitted for the purpose of providing the communication service. HAPP does not intentionally record or store voice conversations.

Microphone access is used only to enable the application's voice communication functionality.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by HAPP is used to:

  • Authenticate users and maintain their accounts.
  • Display user profiles and names.
  • Show recent user interactions.
  • Manage room participation and availability.
  • Establish and maintain voice communication.
  • Maintain the functionality and security of the application.

4. Third-Party Services

HAPP uses third-party services to provide its functionality. These services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

  • Google Sign-In: Used for user authentication.
  • Firebase Realtime Database: Used to store user profiles, interaction information, room participation information and temporary signalling data.
  • WebRTC: Used to facilitate real-time voice communication.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of these third-party service providers.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Account and profile information may be retained while your account remains active or for as long as necessary to provide the application's services.

Room participation information is temporary and is removed when a participant leaves or disconnects, subject to the operation of the underlying services.

Signalling information used to establish voice connections is cleared as part of the room cleanup process.

6. Data Sharing

HAPP does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by the third-party service providers described in this policy where necessary to operate the application.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or valid legal process.

7. Data Security

We rely on the security mechanisms provided by our infrastructure and third-party service providers to protect information.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Permissions

HAPP may request access to your device's microphone to provide voice communication.

You can manage application permissions through your device settings. Denying microphone permission will prevent voice communication from functioning.

9. Children's Privacy

HAPP is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without the necessary consent required by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through HAPP inappropriately, please contact us.
