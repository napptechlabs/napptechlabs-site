HAPP Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026
HAPP is developed by Napp Labs. This Privacy Policy explains what information is used when you use HAPP.
Account information
HAPP uses Google Sign-In for authentication. When you sign in, information associated with your Google account, including your user identifier, display name and email address, is used to create and maintain your HAPP profile.
User information
HAPP stores information necessary to provide its services, including:
→ User identifier
→ Display name
→ Email address
→ Recent user interactions
Room information
HAPP stores temporary room participation information to indicate whether users are currently present in a room. This information is removed when users leave or disconnect, subject to the operation of the underlying services.
Voice communication
HAPP uses WebRTC technology to facilitate real-time voice communication between participants. Microphone access is required for voice communication. HAPP does not intentionally record or store voice conversations.
Third-party services
HAPP uses third-party services provided by Google, including:
→ Google Sign-In — user authentication.
→ Firebase Realtime Database — user profiles, interactions, room presence and signalling information.
→ WebRTC — real-time voice communication.
These services may process information in accordance with their respective privacy policies.
Data sharing
Napp Labs does not sell your personal information or share it with third parties for advertising purposes. Information may be processed by service providers as necessary to operate HAPP or disclosed where required by applicable law.
Data storage
Account and profile information may be retained while your account remains active. Temporary room participation and signalling information is removed as part of the room cleanup process.
Permissions used
→ Internet — required for authentication, database access and communication.
→ Microphone — required for voice communication.
Data deletion
You may request information about your account or request deletion of associated personal information by contacting us. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable data protection laws.
